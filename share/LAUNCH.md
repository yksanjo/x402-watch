## X / Twitter

> You gave your agent a wallet. x402 lets it pay per call — for data, inference, RPC, signals. Great, until it's spending without anyone watching.
>
> **x402-watch** is the layer a human keeps *next to* the agent:
>
> → live tape of every `402 → clear → sign → settle`
> → per-call + daily USD caps + merchant allowlist, enforced *before* it signs
> → one kill switch that stops all settlement
>
> Read-only. Never holds keys. Out of the payment path — if it's down, payments still work, you just lose oversight.
>

## Telegram ($SOAG / agent community)

> 🪤 new build: **x402-watch**
>
> x402 lets an agent settle value on its own — pay per call for data, inference, RPC. The rail is solved (Coinbase, the facilitators). The missing piece is the thing you keep *next to* the agent: a live view of what it's spending, a policy it can't exceed, and a switch that stops it.
>
> x402-watch is that layer. It never holds keys and never signs — it watches every payment's lifecycle, clears or denies each one against your caps + allowlist, and gives you one button to halt all settlement.
>
> Same discipline as Conductor, pointed at money instead of code.
>
> Watch it live (autonomous buyer, sim settlement): x402.musicailab.com
> Code: github.com/yksanjo/x402-watch
